Privacy Policy for DailyGiggle

Last updated: July 26, 2026

DailyGiggle ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by DigitProdAT. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using DailyGiggle, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

DailyGiggle does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal information. There is no backend server: the jokes you've read, your favorite categories, and your notification preferences are stored only on your device using local storage, and are never transmitted to us or to any third party we control.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

DailyGiggle is supported by advertising served through Google AdMob. To show ads (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads), AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, time zone)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies. Depending on your region and device settings, you may be able to limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier from your device's system settings.

3. Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder notification, the time and day(s) you choose are stored locally on your device to schedule it. This data is not sent anywhere; it is only used by the App itself to know when to show the local notification.

4. Children's Privacy

DailyGiggle is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App's advertising is configured for a general audience, not for child-directed treatment.

5. Data Retention and Your Choices

Because all app data (read jokes, settings) is stored only on your device, you can erase it at any time by clearing the App's storage from your device's system settings, or by uninstalling the App. You can also delete individual entries, or your entire joke archive, directly from within the App's Archive screen.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at: dailygiggle.digitprodat@gmail.com
